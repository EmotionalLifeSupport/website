## 1. Homepage metadata and search language

### Provisional keyword review

A qualitative review of current UK search results suggests that the clearest, most commonly used language includes:

- **divorce support**;
- **emotional support during divorce**;
- **support through divorce**;
- **rebuilding life after divorce**; and
- **divorce coach**.

Local variants worth testing include **divorce support Bournemouth**, **divorce support Dorset** and **divorce support online UK**.

**Important limitation:** This is a search-results review, not search-volume research. It shows the language currently used by searchers, publishers and competing services, but it does not establish how many people search for each phrase. Before finalising a long-term SEO strategy, genuine volume and competition data should be checked in Google Keyword Planner, Google Search Console once data is available, or another SEO research tool.

“Divorce coach” appears frequently in the market, but it should not be made the main description of Emotional Life Support unless Chris wants to identify as a divorce coach. Clear and accurate language is more important than forcing a potentially misleading keyword into the headline.

## 8. One-off two-hour session and price decision

No campaign discount will appear on the main website. Any future campaign offer and its reference-price claim will be reviewed separately.

### Proposed offer copy

| Existing copy | Round 2 proposed copy |
| --- | --- |
| No equivalent offer currently exists. The website moves directly from a free hour to £1,200 per month. | **A one-off session**<br><br>**One-Off Two-Hour Support Session**<br><br>Two uninterrupted hours focused entirely on what is happening in your life and what would help now.<br><br>Book a single session whenever you need it, with no monthly commitment and no requirement to continue afterwards.<br><br>**CTA:** Book a two-hour support session |

### Price choice

- **£200 including VAT (£100 per hour):** lower barrier for someone taking a first paid step, but a smaller price difference from monthly support.
- **£300 including VAT (£150 per hour):** makes the value of committing to regular monthly support clearer, but creates a larger jump from the free call.

**Recommendation:** Use **£300 including VAT** if the deliberate strategy is for monthly clients to receive roughly twice as much support time for the same effective hourly cost. Use **£200 including VAT** if the one-off session is primarily intended as an accessible first paid step. Neither option should be published until Chris makes this commercial decision.

- [ ] Approve the offer name and wording
- [ ] Set the website price at £200 including VAT
- [x] Set the website price at £300 including VAT
