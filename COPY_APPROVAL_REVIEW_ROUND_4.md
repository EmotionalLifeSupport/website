### Why this version is different

This version leads with the commercial outcome Chris described: preserving a busy client's capacity to perform at work, be present with their family and look after themselves. The administrative services support that outcome rather than becoming the headline.

It does not promise that every communication can be delegated. Before offering former-partner communication, the service agreement, privacy notice, professional-indemnity cover and operating process should be reviewed by a solicitor.
