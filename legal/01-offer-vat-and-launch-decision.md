# Emotional Life Support — offer, VAT and launch decision

Status: working launch specification, 19 August 2026. This is commercial and compliance planning, not a substitute for advice from a UK VAT adviser, consumer solicitor or insurer.

## Contracting business

- Legal entity: DM4L LTD
- Trading name: Emotional Life Support
- Company number: 13764105
- Registered office: 30 London Road, Sawbridgeworth, England, CM21 9JS
- Launch market: consumers aged 18+ resident in England and Wales
- Service: non-clinical emotional and practical support through separation, divorce and rebuilding

## Exact launch journey

1. A free 60-minute support and suitability conversation. This is not crisis care or a clinical assessment. The first few minutes confirm identity, location, scope, privacy and immediate-safety limitations.
2. If suitable, DM4L LTD sends a written offer summary, terms, privacy notice, cancellation form and any explicit-consent requests.
3. The client accepts in writing. No paid contract is formed merely by using the website or booking the free call.
4. The client either waits until the 14-day cancellation period has ended or separately and expressly requests an early start.
5. The engagement is a rolling monthly agreement. It renews and is billed on the same calendar date each month unless either party gives at least 30 days' written notice to end it.

## VAT answer

At 20% VAT, a £1,000 VAT-inclusive consumer price contains £166.67 VAT and leaves £833.33 before other costs. That is a 16.67% reduction from the sticker price, not a 20% reduction. To retain £1,000 before other costs, the consumer price is £1,200 including VAT; to retain £2,500 it is £3,000. The chosen £10,000 VAT-inclusive concierge price leaves £8,333.33 before other costs.

Creating a new company or subsidiary to sell substantially the same service is not a safe shortcut. A genuinely separate company is a separate taxable person, but HMRC can direct registration where business activities have been artificially separated. It considers financial, economic and organisational links. A subsidiary sharing the founder, brand, website, customers, staff, premises, systems or costs would display many of those links. A VAT group is one taxable person and therefore does not remove VAT from consumer sales.

Legitimate items to ask a VAT adviser to model are:

- whether DM4L LTD can deregister because its total taxable turnover is expected to remain below the current £88,000 deregistration threshold (not merely this trading activity);
- standard VAT accounting versus the Flat Rate Scheme, if eligible. The scheme does not alter the VAT charged to customers and service businesses with few qualifying goods may be “limited cost businesses” at 16.5% of VAT-inclusive turnover;
- the amount of recoverable input VAT under ordinary accounting; and
- price architecture and delivery cost, rather than artificial separation.

Do not describe this service as VAT-exempt healthcare. On the current facts it is expressly non-clinical and is not supplied as health treatment by an appropriately registered health professional.

## Launch positioning

The public site should lead with the free conversation, not an online checkout. Paid prices must always say “including VAT” and “per month”, alongside the rolling monthly renewal and 30 days' written notice terms. Do not promise confidentiality without stating the serious-safety, safeguarding and legal limits.
