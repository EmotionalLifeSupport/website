# Launch checklist

The website can be publicly promoted only when every launch-critical item below is complete. “Done in code” means implemented in the current local website draft; it does not mean legally approved.

## Done in the website draft

- [x] exact registered business name, company number and registered office in footer;
- [x] VAT-inclusive consumer prices and rolling monthly terms with 30 days' written notice;
- [x] concierge scope and non-emergency boundaries;
- [x] non-clinical and emergency boundaries;
- [x] urgent and specialist support routes;
- [x] privacy, cookie and service-term drafts;
- [x] outcome disclaimer and credential non-endorsement wording;
- [x] global HubSpot tracker and embedded meeting code removed;
- [x] sensitive-data warning before external booking; and
- [x] desktop/mobile build and rendered-page checks.

## Business details required

- [ ] public service/privacy email;
- [ ] VAT registration number;
- [ ] administrative contact hours and response target;
- [ ] final payment method; and
- [ ] final complaints and cancellation contact.

## Suppliers and privacy

- [ ] choose and document hosting/CDN, HubSpot booking, email/calendar, video, payment, secure notes and messaging providers;
- [ ] controller–processor agreements and international-transfer safeguards checked;
- [ ] HubSpot form reduced to minimum fields and retention configured;
- [ ] DPIA completed and signed off;
- [ ] Article 9 explicit-consent record tested;
- [ ] Article 10/Schedule 1 condition and Appropriate Policy Document approved where required;
- [ ] ICO fee checker completed and fee paid if due;
- [ ] MFA, encrypted devices, access controls, backups, deletion and 72-hour breach procedure tested; and
- [ ] browser audit confirms no optional cookies or trackers before consent.

## Consumer contract

- [ ] consumer solicitor approves terms, offer summary, component values, early-start wording and model cancellation form;
- [ ] durable email/PDF pack and version/timestamp records tested;
- [ ] fair cancellation/refund workflow tested;
- [ ] hosted payment and compliant VAT invoice tested; and
- [ ] complaints/ADR route approved and published.

## Safeguarding and delivery

- [ ] safeguarding professional approves model and thresholds;
- [ ] named safeguarding lead and cover;
- [ ] training completed and documented;
- [ ] supervision arrangement active;
- [ ] referral directory and emergency contacts current;
- [ ] incident, complaint, lone-working and escalation rehearsals completed;
- [ ] professional indemnity, public liability and cyber/data insurance confirm the exact service; and
- [ ] reasonable-adjustment process and accessible venue/call options ready.

## Promotion

- [ ] evidence file for lived-experience and volunteer/training claims;
- [ ] permission/name-use rules checked with named organisations;
- [ ] no sensitive-category ad targeting or client-list audience uploads;
- [ ] every ad shows material limitations and VAT-inclusive price where price-led;
- [ ] testimonial consent and representativeness process approved; and
- [ ] final human review of each ad/post/email against the promotional-claims matrix.
