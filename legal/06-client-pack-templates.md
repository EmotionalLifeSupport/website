## Complaint acknowledgement standard

Publish a complete complaints procedure after insurer/solicitor review. Operationally: acknowledge within two working days; identify a person not directly conflicted where possible; preserve records; provide a reasoned response target (for example, 14 calendar days); explain any delay; state the escalation/ADR route; and never penalise a client for raising a concern.
